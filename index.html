About

DJ Maros is a Valencia-based DJ playing schranz and acid techno. Originally active in Budapest, he has performed at club nights and private events, developing a precise, fast-paced mixing style.

Lately the sets have pushed harder — looped, distorted percussion and relentless kicks up to 170 BPM, with 303 lines still cutting through. Built for peak-time floors and warehouse rooms that don't want a breather.
